# Voice Standard — Croftspan Site

Derived from the live site (source of truth) and the still-valid voice attributes from the brand system. The site's actual copy is the reference, not the old agency-model writing samples.

## Voice Attributes

These five attributes are the voice. They don't change.

1. **Direct.** Say the thing. No throat-clearing, no hedging. "Nobody translated your processes into something AI can run" not "companies may be experiencing challenges with AI adoption."
2. **Confident without performing.** The specificity of the recommendations shows competence. Never claim expertise. Show it. "15 years of building products from zero to exit" not "our world-class team."
3. **Specific.** Real numbers, real names, real outcomes. "$650M in exits" not "significant experience." "Blockfolio ($150M acquisition)" not "successful products."
4. **Honest about limits.** "We'll tell you honestly whether we can help." If something is outside scope, say so.
5. **Warm, not cute.** Contractions. "You" and "your" over "we" and "our." No exclamation marks. No emoji enthusiasm. Human tone.

## Sentence Patterns (from the live site)

**Lead with the customer's reality:**
- "Your team has workflows that work."
- "You've looked at AI tools, maybe tried a few."
- "Every new project felt like starting from scratch."

**Name the transformation, not the feature:**
- "Your workflows run on AI automation with built-in review gates."
- "New people produce quality work from day one."
- "The system gets better over time, not bigger."

**Short paragraphs. Two to three sentences. Then move on.**

**Fragments for emphasis:**
- "Consistent output. Reviewable work. Any domain."
- "Not a tool subscription. Not a course."

## Copy Rules

- No em dashes. Use periods.
- "You" and "your" more than "we" and "our."
- Numbers and names over vague quantities.
- Short sentences. Declarative. If it exceeds 25 words, split it.
- Contractions always. "We've" not "we have." "It's" not "it is."
- No exclamation marks in site copy. Ever.
- Oxford comma always.
- Sentence case for headings. "How we get there" not "How We Get There."

## The Customer Is the Hero

Every section on the site describes what changes in the customer's organization. Not what Croftspan offers.

- "Your team ships consistent, reviewable work" (services hero)
- "Your workflows, running on AI automation" (homepage hero)
- "Your team deserves processes that actually work" (homepage CTA)

The founder's track record is proof of capability, not a credential wall. It connects to the customer's problem: "That same discipline, applied to AI operations."

## AI-Native Organization Voice

When writing about the team or organization model:
- The team roles are real. Don't qualify them. Don't apologize.
- "Real roles. AI-powered. Human-directed."
- The operating model is a competitive advantage, not something to explain.
- "Croftspan is the proof that it works."

## Banned Phrases

"Leverage," "synergy," "cutting-edge," "world-class," "best-in-class," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unlock," "empower," "drive results," "holistic," "ecosystem," "paradigm," "proven." Any phrase that could appear on any consultancy site without modification.

## Banned Patterns

- "We offer / we provide" (say what changes for the customer instead)
- "Our expertise" (show it, don't claim it)
- Feature-first copy ("we offer AI operational consulting")
- Credential dumps without connecting to the customer's problem
- Hedging: "might," "could potentially," "we believe that"
