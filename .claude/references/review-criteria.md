# Review Criteria — Croftspan Site

Extracted from team quality bars and standards quality gates. Used by `gigo:verify` during review.

## Spec Compliance Criteria

*Did we build the right thing?*

- [ ] Every page answers in 10 seconds: what problem this solves, who it's for, why trust the person behind it (Positioning Strategist quality bar)
- [ ] Copy describes what changes in the customer's organization, not what Croftspan offers (Transformation Framework)
- [ ] Every claim traces to something specific and real (Credibility test)
- [ ] Customer is the hero, Croftspan is the guide (Transformation test)
- [ ] Each page has exactly one primary action (Conversion Architect quality bar)
- [ ] Landing to contact is never more than two clicks (Conversion Architect quality bar)
- [ ] Founder's track record connects to customer's problem, not displayed as credentials (Transformation test)

## Craft Review Criteria

*Is the work well-built?*

- [ ] Every sentence passes "so what?" (Standards quality gate)
- [ ] Every sentence passes "would a stranger keep reading?" (Voice Keeper quality bar)
- [ ] Every sentence passes "would a skeptical engineer call bullshit?" (Voice Keeper quality bar)
- [ ] No em dashes, no "proven," no jargon, no banned phrases (Brand rules)
- [ ] Voice is direct, confident without performing, specific, honest, warm not cute (Brand standard)
- [ ] Mobile-first, performant, minimal JavaScript (Conversion Architect)
- [ ] No sliders, carousels, modals, or decorative elements that don't serve conversion (Conversion Architect anti-patterns)
- [ ] A stranger (not a friend) would keep reading past the first paragraph (Stranger test)

## Challenger Criteria

*Will this approach succeed?*

- [ ] Would removing the persona language change the output? If not, the team added nothing (Overwatch)
- [ ] Could this copy appear on any consultancy site without modification? If so, it's generic (Overwatch)
- [ ] Does the page structure serve the micro-decision sequence? Every element earns the next scroll (Positioning Strategist)
- [ ] Has the gap between substance and presentation reopened anywhere? (Overwatch core mandate)
- [ ] Are domain claims scoped correctly? Separated "validated" from "used in production" (Credibility rules)
