# Brand — Croftspan Site

## Philosophy

Draws from the Croftspan brand system (voice guide, messaging framework, visual identity). Enforced by the PE credibility review standard: honesty as a credibility strategy.

## The Standard

Voice is direct, confident without performing, specific, honest about limits, warm not cute. Every word earns its place. Read aloud. If it sounds like marketing, rewrite it.

## Language Rules

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| proven | validated, battle-tested, or just show the results |
| assembled context | expert knowledge |
| context engineering | making AI output better |
| two-tier architecture | keeps your project lean |
| em dashes | periods |
| we offer / we provide | you get / your team gets / what changes |
| our expertise | (show it, don't claim it) |

## Banned Phrases

"Leverage," "synergy," "cutting-edge," "world-class," "best-in-class," "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unlock," "empower," "drive results," "holistic," "ecosystem," "paradigm." Any phrase that could appear on any consultancy site without modification.

## Visual Identity

- **Colors:** Forge (#1C1917), Vellum (#F5F0E8), Stone (#57534E), Copperfield (#B45309), Slate (#334155), Survey Red (#DC2626), Lichen (#16A34A), Chalk (#D6D3D1)
- **Typography:** DM Serif Display (headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (code)
- **Icons:** Line icons, 24x24 grid, 1.5px stroke, Stone/Copperfield colors
- **Motion:** 150-300ms transitions, purposeful not decorative

## When to Go Deeper

When writing any copy, check against the full voice guide at `~/croftspan/croftspan-app/executive/brand/voice-guide.md`.
When designing layouts or components, check the style guide at `~/croftspan/croftspan-app/executive/brand/style-guide.md`.
When choosing messaging angles, check `~/croftspan/croftspan-app/executive/brand/messaging-framework.md`.
