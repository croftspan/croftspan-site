The engagement

We translate your workflows
into AI automation

Croftspan audits your existing workflows, translates them into AI automation, trains your team to run the new processes, and stays on retainer for ongoing optimization. Not a tool subscription. Not a course. Hands-on operational setup for any domain.

The process

What changes at each phase

1

Audit

We map your existing workflows end to end. How your team operates today, what processes are manual, where output varies, where bottlenecks live. We look at any AI tooling you've already adopted and assess what's working and what's not. You get an honest picture of where you are and what's possible.

2

Build

We translate your workflows into AI automation. Expert teams assembled for your specific domain. Planning pipelines that produce specs your team can review and trust. Quality gates that catch real issues before they ship. Automatic maintenance that keeps the system lean over time. Your processes now run on AI rails with the same checks your team already relies on.

3

Train

We train your team to run the automated processes themselves. The goal is independence. Your people learn how to maintain the system, extend it for new projects, and keep it sharp. They don't need us to operate it. They need us to set it up right.

4

Retainer

We stay on to optimize. As your needs evolve, we refine the processes. As your team grows, we scale the automation with them. The system gets sharper over time, not bigger. If there comes a point where you don't need us anymore, we'll tell you.

Outcomes

What you get

Consistent output

Your automated workflows produce the same quality every time. Not great on Tuesday and garbage on Thursday. Consistent across sessions, projects, and team members.

Reviewable work

Every piece of output goes through structured review gates. Your team knows exactly what to check. Reviews find almost nothing to fix because the upstream process caught it.

Processes that scale

New team members produce quality work from their first session. The processes don't depend on tribal knowledge or one person knowing the right way to do things.

Systems that get sharper

Built-in audit and maintenance means your automation improves over time, not bloats. Every cycle leaves the system in better shape than it found it.
