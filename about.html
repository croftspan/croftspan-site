The methodology

Walk in. Figure it out.
Build the process. Scale it.

Croftspan was built on a pattern: walk into something new, figure out how it works, build the operational process, then scale it. That pattern has been applied across mobile publishing, cryptocurrency, NFT marketplaces, and AI products. It produced $650M in exits and products that shipped on time with process, not luck.

Now that same methodology is applied to AI operations. Not just for engineering teams. For any organization with workflows that could run faster, more consistently, and more reviewably with AI automation.

The approach

Start with the workflow, not the tools

Most companies trying to adopt AI start with the tools. They install something, write some prompts, and hope the output improves. It doesn't. The prompts grow. The configuration contradicts itself. The output gets worse, not better.

We start with the workflow. What does your team actually do? What are the steps, the review gates, the handoffs? Then we translate those human processes into AI automation that preserves the quality checks your team already relies on. The AI runs the workflow. Your team reviews the output.
